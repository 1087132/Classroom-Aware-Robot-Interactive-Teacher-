Classroom-Aware Interactive Robot Teacher

A smart teaching assistant that tracks engagement in real time, runs quizzes and mini-games, answers on-topic questions with a constrained LLM, and keeps the teacher in control - all on your local network.

📱 Add to home screen

For quick access on this device, tap your browser's share button and choose Add to Home Screen.

Quick demo (2 minutes)

1. Open the Teacher Dashboard and press Start Lesson.
2. Open the Robot Face and Projector in separate windows.
3. Students scan the QR code (or open the student URL) and join.
4. Press Next to teach and launch questions - watch answers and live engagement scores roll in.
5. Press End Lesson, then review the session in Student Tracking.
